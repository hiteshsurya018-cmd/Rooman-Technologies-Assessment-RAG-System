# Expected Outputs

The exact wording can vary because the answer is generated by FLAN-T5, but the behavior should match these expectations.

## Supported Question

Question:

```text
What is paracetamol commonly used for?
```

Expected behavior:

- The answer mentions pain relief and/or fever reduction.
- The answer includes an inline citation such as `[paracetamol_usage.pdf | p.1 | chunk_001]`.
- Retrieved evidence includes a chunk from `paracetamol_usage.pdf`.

## Safety Question

Question:

```text
What safety risk is mentioned for excess paracetamol?
```

Expected behavior:

- The answer mentions overdose, excess use, or liver harm.
- The answer includes an inline citation from `paracetamol_safety.pdf`.
- The source chunk display shows the text used for grounding.

## Multi-Document Question

Question:

```text
Compare the usage notes and safety notes for paracetamol.
```

Expected behavior:

- The answer uses evidence from both sample PDFs when retrieval finds both relevant.
- The retrieved evidence panel shows chunks from both documents.
- Citations identify filename, page, and chunk id.

## Unsupported Question

Question:

```text
Does the document mention treatment for broken bones?
```

Expected behavior:

```text
I could not find sufficient information in the provided documents to answer this question.

Reason:
No sufficiently relevant evidence was retrieved.
```

The response should not invent treatment guidance.
